JB Kelly presents 2 Alden Drive, an extended semi-detached family home in a highly sought after and mature location. The property features a large side garden, offering valuable outdoor space and scope for a further extension or potential development (subject to the necessary planning permission).

An entrance porch leads to the hallway, with a sitting room to the front and double doors through to a large kitchen dining room to the rear with a bright conservatory opening onto the south-facing rear garden. A side extension provides a utility room and a ground-floor shower room with WC. Upstairs, there are three generous bedrooms and a family bathroom.

The side garden gives the next owner several options: retain it as a larger garden, create more space for outdoor living, or explore its future potential. Together with the south-facing rear aspect, the additional outdoor space is a particularly unique aspect of this home. A driveway to the front provides off-street parking for multiple cars and there is also a block built storage shed to the rear.

Bayside Square Shopping Centre, local schools, sports facilities and parks are all nearby. Bayside DART Station and regular bus services provide convenient access to the city, while the coastal promenade and cycle track offer an easy route to the seafront.

2 Alden Drive offers a rare opportunity to acquire a solid family home on a generous corner site. Contact JB Kelly on 01 839 3400 to arrange a viewing.
